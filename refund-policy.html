Event tickets

Cancel up to 48 hours before the event for a full refund. After that we refund only if we can resell your seat.

Private dining deposits

Deposits are refundable up to 14 days before the event.

Pickup and delivery

You can cancel an order until the kitchen accepts it. If something is wrong with your order, tell us within 24 hours.

How refunds are paid

To the card or method you paid with, within 5–10 working days.
