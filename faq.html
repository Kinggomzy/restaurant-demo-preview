FAQs

Quick answers.

Booking and opening-hours answers come straight from our own settings, so they are always current.

How do I book a table?
Use Book a table. You can book up to 90 days ahead, for up to 12 guests online, with at least 15 minutes' notice. For larger groups, send a private dining enquiry.
Can I cancel or change a booking?
Yes. Cancel free up to 24 hours before your time. Later cancellations and no-shows may be recorded. We hold a table for 15 minutes after your booking time.
Can I join a queue without a booking?
Yes. Use the walk-in queue to join from your phone and see your place in line.
When are you open?
Harbour Street: Monday to Wednesday 12:00–15:00 and 17:30–22:30, Thursday until 23:00, Friday 12:00–23:00, Saturday 11:00–23:00, Sunday 11:00–21:00. Hours for every room are on Visit us.
Where are you?
Harbour Street, 1 Quay Road, Bristol BS1 4AB. Directions are on the Visit us page.
Can you cater for allergies and diets?
Every dish shows its dietary tags and allergens where we have confirmed them. Always tell your server about an allergy.
Do you host private events?
Yes. Send a private dining enquiry with your date and guest count and we will send a quotation.
How do event tickets work?
Reserve tickets on the Events page and pay at the venue. Your ticket has a code to show at the door. Refunds follow the refund policy.
How do gift cards and loyalty work?
Gift cards are used at payment and their balance can be checked online. Loyalty points are earned on eligible visits; see My data.
Are dogs welcome?
Yes, on the Harbour Street terrace and in the bar. Water bowls are on us.
Do you have high chairs and a children's menu?
Yes, at all three rooms. Children under 12 eat for £6 at weekend lunch.
How do I contact you?
Use the Contact page, or phone 0117 496 0321.
