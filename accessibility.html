This website

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Every page works with a keyboard and a screen reader, text can be enlarged to 200%, and motion is reduced if you ask your device to.

Our rooms

  • Step-free entrance and accessible toilet at Harbour Street and the Rooftop Bar (lift)
  • A ramp for the three steps at Old Mill
  • Large-print and allergen menus at every room
  • Assistance dogs are always welcome

Tell us

If anything is hard to use, please contact us. We reply within two working days.
