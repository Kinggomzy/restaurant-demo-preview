Welcome to The Corner Table

What the boats bring in, we cook tonight.

We cook what the day boats and West Country farms bring in each morning. Pasta is rolled by hand before lunch, the beef shin goes into the pot at ten and comes out at seven, and the wine list leans on small growers we have visited ourselves.
